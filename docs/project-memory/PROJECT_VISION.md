# Project vision

## Purpose
**Penelope** ("AI Car Companion") is a voice-first assistant for drivers. It helps people with little or no car knowledge understand and look after their car. It is growing into a complete hands-free driving companion: vehicle diagnostics, music, messages, calls and, later, navigation.

## Target users
- **Everyday drivers first:** plain language, no jargon, one-tap actions, voice by default.
- **DIY** and **Advanced** users get more detail and control in the same app.
- **Knowledge level** (Everyday / DIY) and **home layout** (Easy / Customizable) are separate settings.

## Long-term goals
1. A trustworthy car expert in your pocket: honest, safe, and never inventing readings.
2. Fully hands-free while driving, by voice through the car's speakers.
3. Diagnostics that work with common, affordable OBD-II adapters.
4. **Android first, then a native iOS app** sharing the same product rules.

## Non-negotiables
- Two-way, continuous voice conversation (no placeholders).
- Never present sample or guessed data as real.
- Read-only vehicle access by default; nothing changes the car automatically.
- Privacy: no transcripts, VINs, contacts or keys in logs, crash reports or the AI.
- Driver safety: voice first; messages and calls need spoken confirmation.

## Feature priorities (current order)
1. **Prove what's built on real hardware:** S26 Ultra, adapter, 2011 528i.
2. **Hands-free driving:** music (H1), then messages and calls.
3. **Richer dashboards** for DIY and Advanced users.
4. **Deeper diagnostics:** freeze-frame, VIN check, mechanic report.
5. **iOS app.**
